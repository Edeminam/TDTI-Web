Role Summary

The Programs Officer will support the planning, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of TechRise DTI’s digital-skills, education, youth-empowerment, and community-development programs. The officer will coordinate program activities, manage stakeholder relationships, prepare reports, and contribute to grant writing and fundraising efforts that support the organisation’s mission and sustainability.

Key Responsibilities

  • Plan, coordinate, and implement programs according to approved objectives, timelines, and budgets.
  • Develop work plans, implementation schedules, program frameworks, and supporting documentation.
  • Coordinate digital-skills training, webinars, workshops, scholarships, community outreach, and youth-development initiatives.
  • Research relevant local and international grant opportunities, funding calls, and partnership prospects.
  • Write compelling grant proposals, concept notes, letters of inquiry, and funding applications.
  • Prepare project narratives, logical frameworks, theories of change, budgets, sustainability plans, and supporting grant documents.
  • Maintain a grant calendar and track deadlines, submission requirements, donor feedback, and reporting obligations.
  • Collaborate with the leadership and finance teams to prepare program budgets and monitor the use of funds.
  • Monitor program implementation and measure progress against agreed deliverables and key performance indicators.
  • Collect and analyse program data, participant feedback, outcomes, lessons learned, and success stories.
  • Prepare periodic program, impact, donor, and management reports.
  • Maintain relationships with beneficiaries, trainers, volunteers, partners, donors, government institutions, and community stakeholders.
  • Support partnership development, sponsorship acquisition, donor engagement, and resource mobilisation.
  • Ensure compliance with organisational policies, donor requirements, safeguarding standards, and relevant regulations.
  • Identify program risks and recommend suitable mitigation measures.
  • Represent TechRise DTI at stakeholder meetings, conferences, community engagements, and other relevant events.
  • Support the preparation of communication materials highlighting program activities, achievements, and impact.
  • Perform other program-related responsibilities assigned by management.

Qualifications and Experience

  • Bachelor’s degree in Development Studies, Project Management, Public Administration, Social Sciences, International Relations, Communications, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • A minimum of two to three years of relevant experience in program coordination, nonprofit management, community development, education, youth empowerment, or digital-skills initiatives.
  • Demonstrable experience in grant research, grant writing, proposal development, and donor reporting.
  • Experience preparing concept notes, project proposals, implementation plans, budgets, and monitoring frameworks.
  • Previous experience working with an NGO, nonprofit organisation, social enterprise, or development-focused institution is desirable.
  • Familiarity with donor requirements, funding application procedures, and project-compliance standards.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, project-management platforms, and online collaboration tools.

Required Skills and Competencies

  • Excellent grant writing, research, proposal development, and report-writing skills.
  • Strong program and project-management abilities.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong stakeholder-engagement and relationship-management skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple programs, priorities, and deadlines.
  • Good analytical, monitoring, evaluation, and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to accurate documentation.
  • Ability to work independently and collaborate effectively with a remote or multidisciplinary team.
  • Creativity, initiative, accountability, and a passion for technology, education, youth development, and social impact.

Key Performance Indicators

Performance will be assessed based on:

  • Timely and successful implementation of assigned programs.
  • Quality and number of grant proposals and funding applications submitted.
  • Grant success rate and value of funding secured.
  • Achievement of program targets and measurable beneficiary outcomes.
  • Accuracy and timeliness of program and donor reports.
  • Quality of stakeholder, beneficiary, and partner engagement.
  • Compliance with approved budgets, donor requirements, and organisational policies.
  • Effectiveness of program monitoring, documentation, and impact reporting.

Compensation

The Programs Officer will receive a monthly salary of ₦150,000, subject to the organisation’s employment terms, statutory obligations, and performance requirements.
